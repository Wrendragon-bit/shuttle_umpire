Rules applied
  • Rally point: every rally gives a point to the winner.
  • A game goes to 21 and must be won by 2 points; at 29–29 the next point wins (30).
  • Best of 3 games. The winner of a rally serves next.
  • Server stands in the right court when their score is even, left when odd.
  • Doubles: the serving side swaps courts after each point it wins; the receiving side does not swap when it wins the serve.
  • Interval at 11 points; ends change after each game and at 11 in game 3.
  • Shuttle landing on a line is IN. Landing inside = point to the other side; landing outside = point against the side that hit it.

    Put the camera anywhere with the whole court in view: side, corner or behind, low or high. Start the camera, then let the AI find the court (or tap “Calibrate court”) and drag the corners to match the lines.
